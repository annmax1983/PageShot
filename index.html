Fully Local Screenshot Tool

PageShot is a screenshot extension for Chrome and Edge that captures a full page, the visible area or just the part you drag over. Take the shot, annotate it if you need to, then copy it or save a clean PNG. Everything happens inside your browser, with no forced watermark and no upload. No account, no ads and no tracking. It is made for people who want a fast screenshot tool that does not turn a quick capture into a cloud workflow.

01

Everything it does

The core features at a glance.

01

Three capture modes: whole page, visible area or a dragged selection.

02

Copy the result to the clipboard and paste it straight into a chat, email or document.

03

Save a PNG to a PageShot folder inside Downloads, with a timestamped filename.

04

Annotate with rectangles, ellipses, arrows, text and mosaic blur.

05

Five annotation colors: red, blue, green, black and white.

06

Undo and clear controls keep the editor forgiving.

Also included

  • Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+Shift+V for visible area and copy, Ctrl+Shift+F for full page and download, Ctrl+Shift+S for selection.
  • Right-click menu with all three capture modes, so you never need to open the popup.
  • A toast after each capture offers Copy, Download and Edit.
  • Sticky headers and footers are removed from full-page shots.
  • Six interface languages.
  • No watermark on your screenshots.

02

Free vs Premium

The free tier is a complete tool, not a trial. Premium is optional.

Free

full-page, visible-area and selection capture; clipboard copy; PNG download; the annotation editor with rectangles, ellipses, arrows, text and mosaic; all five colors; undo and clear; keyboard shortcuts; the right-click menu; sticky-element removal; lazy-load handling and all six interface languages. No watermark, no daily capture limit and no counter.

Premium

PDF export from the annotation editor is the only paid feature. One licence key activates on two browsers, and removing the licence from one browser frees that slot again.

The licence is optional. PageShot keeps working offline and asks the licence server for a check about every two hours; if the check fails, nothing is locked.

Private by design

Your data stays on your device. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Read exactly what is stored and when the extension uses the network.
