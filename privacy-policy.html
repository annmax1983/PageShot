PageShot Privacy Policy

PageShot does NOT collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data. All screenshot processing happens fully locally on your device. The only network request the extension makes is the optional Premium license check described in Section 4.

01Data Collection

PageShot does not collect any personal information, browsing data, screenshots, or usage analytics. We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or telemetry of any kind.

02How PageShot Works

All screenshot operations are performed entirely within your browser:

  • Capture — Screenshots are taken using browser-native APIs (chrome.tabs.captureVisibleTab and Chrome DevTools Protocol)
  • Processing — Image stitching, cropping, and annotation are performed in a local Offscreen Document using the Canvas API
  • Storage — Screenshots are held temporarily in browser memory during processing and are immediately discarded after you copy or download them
  • Output — Images are saved to your local downloads folder or copied to your system clipboard
At no point does any data leave your browser.

04Third-Party Services & License Verification

PageShot does not connect to any third-party services, APIs, CDNs, or external servers, and screenshot handling works entirely offline. The only exception is the optional Premium license: free features make no network request at all. With a license, the extension contacts our license server (api.annmax1983.com) only to activate it, to re-verify it at most once every 2 hours when you open the extension, and to release it. These requests carry your license key, a random install identifier, and basic browser metadata (browser name/version, browser language, time zone, user agent) — never your screenshots, page content, or browsing data. Our license server records the IP address of these requests solely for rate limiting and abuse prevention — never for profiling, advertising, or sharing with third parties.

05Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any data with third parties because we do not collect any data in the first place.

06Children's Privacy

PageShot does not collect any personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13.

07Open Source

PageShot's source code is available for review. You can verify that the extension behaves as described in this privacy policy.

08Data retention & deletion

Your settings and content stay in your browser until you delete them in the extension or uninstall it. Uninstalling removes the extension's local data. Nothing is retained on our servers, because your content is never sent to them.

09Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new date. Material changes will be noted in the extension's release notes.
